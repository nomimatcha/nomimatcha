In plain words要約

We build the site to meet the Israeli accessibility standard (IS 5568, WCAG level AA). It works with a keyboard, a screen reader and large text. A few things aren’t perfect yet and we list them below. If something stops you, write to our accessibility coordinator and we fix it or find another way to serve you.

01Our commitment

Everyone should be able to browse, choose and order without help. We design with that in mind from the start, and we test it rather than assume it.

02The standard we follow

The site is adjusted to the requirements of the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 2013, and to Israeli Standard 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA. We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA where we can.

03What we have done

  • Keyboard. Every link, button and form works with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Esc, in a logical order, and a “Skip to content” link comes first.
  • Focus. Whatever you are on has a clear outline.
  • Screen readers. Pages use proper headings and landmarks, buttons and links have names, icons that carry meaning are labelled, and form fields have labels.
  • Colour and contrast. Text meets AA contrast. We never rely on colour alone to say something.
  • Text size. You can enlarge text up to 200% with your browser without losing content or function.
  • Motion. Moving elements respect the “reduce motion” setting on your device.
  • Languages. Hebrew, English and Japanese text are marked with their language, and the Hebrew pages read right to left.
  • Forms and errors. Errors are described in words and the field is brought into focus.

04Known limitations

We would rather tell you than hide it. These are not fully accessible yet, and we are working on them.

05Using the site your way

  • Zoom with Ctrl or Cmd and + or −. The layout adapts.
  • Press Esc to close the menu or any open panel.
  • Screen reader and voice control users: all controls have text names you can say out loud.
  • Prefer not to use the site? Order by email or WhatsApp and we take the order by hand.

06Physical access

We don’t run a shop open to the public. Orders are delivered to your door or to a pickup point, and meetings at our office are by appointment. Tell us what you need and we arrange it: [Office address and access details].

07Accessibility coordinator

Found something that doesn’t work, or need the content in another form? Write to our coordinator. Say which page, what you were trying to do, and what device or assistive tool you use. We answer within 5 business days.
